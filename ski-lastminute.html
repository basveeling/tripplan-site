The pattern: the western Alps (Savoie, Valais, Aosta) got the big November storms and have the deep base. Next week is dry everywhere — sunny skiing on whatever is already down, which favours the west. Around 5–10 Dec a storm is forecast mainly for northern Austria, which is why Kühtai looks better for windows B and C than for next week. That storm is 10–15 days out: a signal, not a promise.

Before booking

Several stays show “1 left”. Prices move daily this close to departure; re-check each one at the moment of booking.
Kühtai's free pass depends on where you book. The offer is tied to participating properties; confirm the rate includes it.
Hire cars are estimates. Winter tyres are standard on Austrian and Italian rentals in December, but check the rental includes chains for the Cervinia road.
