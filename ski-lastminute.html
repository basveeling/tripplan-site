Will the snow from the big November dumps survive until you ski it? Each panel shows modelled snow depth from 20 Nov to 10 Dec, with your trip shaded. The two strips underneath show how much it thaws each day on the slopes and in the village. The short answer: above about 2,000 m nothing melts next week (it's sunny but cold). The low villages (Sölden, Hintertux, Engelberg, Zermatt) get 5–9 °C afternoons, so their home runs turn icy in the morning and slushy after lunch.

How this is built: Open-Meteo hourly forecasts from two weather models, ICON (DWD, about 2–13 km grid, runs to about 2 Dec) and ECMWF IFS (25 km, runs to 10 Dec). Temperatures are corrected to each altitude shown; snow depth is not. Snow depth is the model grid cell's value, which is why ECMWF often shows the same depth for slopes and village. Thaw is degree-hours above 0 °C per day (the sum of hourly temperatures above freezing, averaged over the two models). A day with 0 means nothing melts at that altitude. Models don't know about snowmaking or grooming, so the pistes are usually in better shape than the natural depth suggests. Treat the snowfall after about 2 Dec as a signal, not a forecast.

Before booking

The Saturday morning margin. The Innsbruck train is due at Amsterdam Centraal at 09:59, which leaves 61 minutes before you need to be home at 11:00. Nightjets often run 30–60+ minutes late on the German leg; I couldn't find punctuality figures for this train. The Basel train is due at 08:59, about 2 hours of buffer. If Saturday 11:00 is a hard deadline, Basel is the safer train, or get off the Innsbruck train at Arnhem (08:43) or Utrecht (09:29) if that's closer to home.
Night-train fares are saver tickets. They're cheap but non-refundable, and 4-berth compartments sell out. Book the train first, then the stay.
Price for 4 adults on hotel sites. Some booking engines keep showing the 2-person price until occupancy is set properly. Alpenrose's Appartement I is €1,696 for four on the Ski free PLUS rate, not the €1,076 it shows for two. Set 4 adults before picking dates, and check AlpinLodges the same way. Alpenrose's Ski free PLUS rate leaves out the arrival and departure days on stays of 4+ nights.
Several stays show “1 left”. Prices move daily this close to departure; re-check each one at the moment of booking.
Kühtai's free pass depends on where you book. The offer is tied to participating properties; confirm the rate includes it.
Hire cars are estimates. Winter tyres are standard on Austrian and Italian rentals in December, but check the rental includes chains for the Cervinia road.
